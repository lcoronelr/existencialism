# The Freedom Academy · Content Worksheet

How to use this:
- Each field has **my recommendation**, **why** I picked it, and a blank **Your version:** line.
- Write your own words after "Your version:". Rough is fine.
- Leave a field empty if you want to keep my recommendation.
- When a section is done, tell Claude "done with Section X" and it goes on the site.

The 5 ideas every section should connect to: **freedom, choice, responsibility, authenticity** (being yourself) and **making your own meaning**.

## Section 7 · Teaching Methods
*Answers: "What teaching methods or strategies would your teachers use?"*

**Headline**
- Recommendation: Teachers guide. Students lead.
- Your version:

**6 methods (name + 1 sentence)**
- Recommendation:
  1. Group talks (Socratic method): the teacher asks questions instead of giving answers.
  2. Outdoor research: students come up with their own questions and go find out.
  3. Real projects: work that matters, like cleaning up a stream.
  4. Journaling: students write about their choices and what happened.
  5. Student-led trips: students plan the trip and make the calls.
  6. Tough choice games: students act out real problems and defend their choice.
- Why: every method puts the student in charge of something.
