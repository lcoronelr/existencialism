Section 1 · Name & Slogan

Your Education.Your Choices.Your Future.

The Freedom Academy is a school where students plan their own future and discover who they really are. Here, you choose what and how you learn, and you take responsibility for those choices.

Create Your Own Path → Explore the Academy

Section 2 · Mission Statement

Our mission is to give students the freedom to explore nature, figure out who they are, and be responsible for all of their choices.

I

Think for yourself

Students ask questions instead of memorizing material.

II

Be creative and show growth

Students keep a personal project or a journal with goals for themselves.

III

Be responsible for your choices

Students understand that they make their own plans.

Section 3 · Our Philosophy

Students decide
who they become.

Existentialism is the idea that you are not born with a purpose. You become who you really are through your own choices, and you are responsible for them.

Section 4 · Physical Environment

A campus with no walls.

Students aren't boxed into one place where they have to follow and learn the same thing as everyone else. The outdoors is also the perfect place to study environmental science, which is what our programs are built around.

Section 7 · Teaching Methods & Strategies

Teachers guide. Students lead.

Group Talks

Teachers ask questions and don't give the answers (the Socratic method).

Outdoor Research

Students study nature by going out and investigating it themselves.

Real Projects

Projects that matter outside of school.

Journaling

Students reflect on the choices they made.

Student-Led Trips

Students plan and lead the trips themselves.

Case Scenarios

Students act out real problems and decide what to do.

The key: students are in charge of every activity.

Section 10 · Teacher Training

Teachers are mentors.

We train our teachers to be a guide for each student, not to interfere with what students do each day. Teachers are only mentors.

What teachers need
DegreeAn environmental science degree, or something related.
LicenseA teaching license.
